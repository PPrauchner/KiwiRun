## 2. Macroeconomic Overview

**A small, open, trade-driven economy.** New Zealand depends heavily on exports of food and fibre, tourism and international education.

**Recent cycle (2024-2026):**
- After a recession in 2024, the RBNZ cut the Official Cash Rate (OCR) from 5.50% to a low of 2.25% by late 2025.
- Early 2026 brought a recovery: GDP grew 0.9% in the March 2026 quarter, one of the best results in three years.
- The 2026 conflict involving Iran triggered a global oil shock. Petrol in New Zealand reached a record of around NZ$ 3.60 per litre in October 2026.
- Inflation jumped to 4.1% in the June 2026 quarter and eased to 3.7% in September, still above target. Economists expect it to rise again by the end of 2026.
- The RBNZ reversed course and raised the OCR to 2.50% in July and 2.75% in September 2026. In October it held at 2.75% in a 4-3 vote, signalling that further tightening is likely. The next decision is on 9 December 2026.
- GDP grew only 0.2% in the June 2026 quarter, but the economy was 2.6% larger than a year earlier.
- The labour market is weak: unemployment reached 5.7% in the September 2026 quarter and annual wage growth slowed to 2.1%.

**Policy agenda:** the government has focused on spending restraint and attracting foreign investment, with initiatives such as **Invest New Zealand**, the **Fast-track Approvals Act** and the **Active Investor Plus visa**.

**Political context:** a general election was held on 7 November 2026. Preliminary results did not give either bloc a clear victory, and official results are due on 27 November 2026. Government formation may take weeks.

## 3. Trade and International Agreements

**Main goods exports:**
1. Dairy: record ~NZ$ 28.6 bn forecast for 2025-26, around 45% of food and fibre revenue
2. Meat and wool: ~NZ$ 14.1 bn (+14%)
3. Horticulture: ~NZ$ 9.5 bn (record kiwifruit and apple volumes)
4. Forestry and wood products
5. Wine and processed foods

**Main markets:** China, Australia, United States, Japan, European Union.

**Trade agreements and developments:**
- **CPTPP** and **RCEP** (Asia-Pacific)
- **NZ-UK FTA** (in force since 2023) and **NZ-EU FTA** (in force since 2024)
- **NZ-India FTA**: signed on 27 April 2026 after only nine months of negotiation. It cuts or removes tariffs on 95% of NZ exports to India and includes a commitment of US$ 20 bn in NZ investment in India over 15 years. It is awaiting ratification.
- **United States**: in February 2026 the US Supreme Court struck down the emergency tariffs (including the 15% applied to NZ goods). NZ exporters may be eligible for up to NZ$ 1 bn in refunds. The US replaced them with a temporary global tariff, so trade policy remains uncertain.

## 4. Technology Sector

**Size and relevance:**
- Technology is one of New Zealand's three largest export sectors, with exports above NZ$ 10 bn.
- It contributes around 8% of GDP and employs over 100,000 people (NZTech estimates).
- Strong areas: SaaS, fintech, healthtech, gaming, creative tech, AgTech and space.

**Leading companies:**

| Company | Area | Highlight |
|---|---|---|
| **Xero** | Accounting SaaS | Global player; acquired US firm Melio in 2025 (~US$ 2.5 bn) |
| **Fisher & Paykel Healthcare** | Medical devices | Global leader in respiratory care |
| **Rocket Lab** | Space | Founded in NZ; Nasdaq-listed (RKLB) |
| **Halter** | AgTech | Valued at US$ 2 bn in March 2026 |
| **Weta FX** | Visual effects | Global reference in film VFX |
| **Grinding Gear Games** | Gaming | Path of Exile; anchors a growing game industry |
| **Serko, Tracksuit, Dawn Aerospace** | Travel tech, marketing SaaS, space | High-growth scale-ups |

**Policy and innovation:**
- **R&D Tax Incentive**: 15% tax credit on eligible R&D spending.
- **Science system reform**: research institutes merged into new Public Research Organisations focused on bioeconomy, earth sciences and advanced technology.
- **National AI Strategy** (2025): light-touch approach focused on business adoption.
- **Game Development Sector Rebate**: 20% rebate for game studios.
- Challenges: low R&D spending compared with the OECD average (~1.5% of GDP), talent shortages and a small domestic market.

## 5. Agriculture and AgTech

### 5.1 Why agriculture matters
- Food and fibre exports reached a record NZ$ 60.4 bn in 2024-25 and are forecast at NZ$ 64.3 bn for the year to June 2026, with records across dairy, red meat and horticulture.
- MPI projects primary exports above NZ$ 70 bn by 2030.
- Around one in seven workers is employed in food and fibre.
- **Fonterra** is the world's largest dairy exporter, with about 30% of global dairy trade.
- **Zespri** markets kiwifruit globally and benefits from record export volumes.

### 5.2 The AgTech ecosystem
- **Livestock management and virtual fencing**
  - **Halter**: solar-powered GPS collars that guide cattle with sound and vibration, replacing physical fences. In March 2026 it raised US$ 220 m (Series E, led by Founders Fund) at a US$ 2 bn valuation, the largest venture round in NZ history. It has sold over 1 million collars to more than 2,000 farms in NZ, Australia and the US.
  - **Gallagher**: global leader in electric fencing, animal weighing and farm security.
  - **Datamars (Tru-Test)**: animal identification and weighing.
- **Genetics and herd improvement**
  - **LIC (Livestock Improvement Corporation)**: dairy genetics, herd testing and data platforms.
  - Breeding of low-methane sheep and cattle.
- **Robotics and automation**
  - **Robotics Plus**: robots for kiwifruit and apple harvesting and packing.
  - **Waikato Milking Systems**: automated milking technology.
- **Farm software and data**
  - **Hectre**: orchard management and fruit-sizing with computer vision.
  - Farm management platforms, soil and water sensors, satellite pasture monitoring.
- **Climate and emissions technology**
  - Agriculture produces about half of New Zealand's gross greenhouse gas emissions, mainly livestock methane.
  - **AgriZeroNZ**: public-private joint venture investing in methane-reducing tools (vaccines, inhibitors, feed additives).
  - **Ag Emissions Centre (NZAGRC)** and **EcoPond** (methane reduction in dairy effluent ponds).
  - The government dropped the plan to price farm emissions and revised the 2050 biogenic methane target to a 14-24% reduction, betting on technology instead of taxation.

### 5.3 Policy for AgTech
- **AgriTech Industry Transformation Plan**: government and industry roadmap to grow AgTech exports.
- **Gene Technology Bill**: would modernise rules on gene editing and GMOs. It advanced in Parliament in 2026 but stalled amid coalition disagreements and was not passed before the November election.
- Challenges: access to capital, farmer adoption costs and dependence on commodity prices.

## 6. Rocket Lab

### 6.1 Company profile
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Founded | 2006, Auckland, New Zealand |
| Founder and CEO | Sir Peter Beck |
| Headquarters | Long Beach, California (USA) |
| Listing | Nasdaq: RKLB |
| NZ operations | Rocket production in Auckland; Launch Complex 1 on the Māhia Peninsula |
| US operations | Launch Complex 2 and 3 (Virginia), spacecraft and component plants |

### 6.2 Launch business
- **Electron**: small orbital rocket made of carbon composite, with 3D-printed Rutherford engines. It is the world's most frequently launched small orbital rocket.
- Record of **21 Electron launches in 2025** with 100% mission success, most of them from Māhia.
- Thanks to this cadence, New Zealand ranked joint third with Russia in total orbital launches in 2025.
- **HASTE**: suborbital version used for hypersonic tests for the US defence sector.
- **Neutron**: medium-lift reusable rocket (~13 tonnes to orbit). In October 2026, the company found a manufacturing issue in a composite tank dome and moved the first launch to **2027**. Even before flying, Neutron already has 16 missions booked.

### 6.3 Space Systems business
- Space Systems (satellites, components, laser communications) generates about 80% of revenue.
- Major contracts include US Space Development Agency satellite programs (including an US$ 816 m contract in late 2025) and NASA missions such as ESCAPADE to Mars.
- Recent acquisitions include Geost and Mynaric (laser communications).

### 6.4 The Iridium deal
- On 29 June 2026, Rocket Lab agreed to acquire satellite operator **Iridium** in a cash-and-stock deal valuing it at about **US$ 8 bn**.
- Iridium brings a global satellite network, valuable L-band spectrum and over 2.5 million subscribers, turning Rocket Lab into an end-to-end space company (launch, satellites and services).
- Closing is expected in mid-2027, subject to approvals.

### 6.5 Financials
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Revenue 2025 | US$ 601.8 m |
| Revenue Q3 2026 | US$ 267.8 m (record, +73% year on year) |
| Q4 2026 guidance | US$ 270-290 m |
| Backlog (30 Sep 2026) | US$ 3.57 bn (record, +52% in one quarter) |
| Launch backlog | 117 launches |
| Net result | Still loss-making (net loss of US$ 79.7 m in Q3 2026) |

### 6.6 Importance for New Zealand
- Placed New Zealand among the few countries with regular orbital launches.
- Drove the creation of the **New Zealand Space Agency** and space legislation.
- Creates high-skill engineering jobs and attracts global investment. Founder Peter Beck is also an early investor in Halter.

## 7. Strengths, Challenges and Outlook

**Strengths**
- Stable institutions, ease of doing business and low corruption.
- Record food and fibre exports and globally competitive AgTech.
- Expanding trade network, now including India.
- High-value tech niches: SaaS, healthtech, space and gaming.

**Challenges**
- Inflation above target driven by fuel prices, with interest rates rising again.
- Unemployment near a decade high and weak household spending.
- High dependence on China and on commodity prices; uncertain US trade policy.
- Pressure to cut agricultural emissions; gene technology reform still pending.

**Outlook (2027)**
- Formation of the new government after official election results.
- Possible further OCR hike in December 2026.
- India FTA ratification and growth in primary exports.
- Neutron's first flight and the closing of the Iridium deal as key milestones for Rocket Lab.
